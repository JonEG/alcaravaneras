General guide to Spain

Beach flags: what each colour means

A general guide to the warning-flag system used on Spanish beaches. It does not confirm which flags are displayed at Las Alcaravaneras.

  • Green flag — swimming permitted

    Conditions are generally favourable and swimming is permitted. Remain cautious even when the green flag is flying.

  • Yellow flag — take care

    There may be waves, currents or other hazards. Take extra care when swimming and follow lifeguard instructions.

  • Red flag — swimming prohibited

    Conditions pose a significant risk. Entering the water is prohibited.

  • White flag with a jellyfish symbol — Jellyfish present

    Warns of jellyfish in the water. This flag may appear with a yellow or red flag; also follow the hazard level indicated by those flags.

Always follow the flags actually displayed on the beach and the lifeguard service’s instructions. This guide explains the general system; it does not imply that every warning is used at Las Alcaravaneras. Sources: Ministry of the Interior, safety guide (PDF) ↗ · Government of Catalonia, beach flag guide ↗.

Before swimming

Beach safety

General advice for enjoying the sea safely.

  • Follow the flags and any restrictions in force.
  • Follow lifeguard instructions.
  • Do not enter the water if conditions are unsafe.
  • Keep a close eye on children at all times.
