Privacy

Cookie policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026.

What cookies are

Cookies are small pieces of data that a website can store in or read from your browser. Similar technologies, such as local storage, may also be used. This page describes the connections and storage that could be verified in the site code; browser and third-party provider behaviour may change.

What this site uses

Essential

The site stores your preferences in your browser’s local storage under the key alcaravaneras.consent, along with the date of your choice and this settings version. It is used to remember and apply your choice for up to six months. The essential category is always active. The code does not create first-party cookies; when Google consent is withdrawn, it attempts to delete Google cookies that can be identified from this domain.

Preferences

This category is off by default. The current code does not use an additional preferences cookie. If a feature that needs one is added, it will be described here before being enabled.

Analytics

The site reserves this category for Google Analytics or other analytics tags that may be configured in the Google Tag Manager container GTM-MM258425. The source code alone does not reveal which tags, GA4 properties, cookies, retention periods or recipients are configured in that container. The container is not loaded until you allow analytics or marketing. Verify the configuration described at the end of this page before publication.

Marketing and advertising

This category is off by default and reserved for advertising or remarketing tags that may be present in GTM. We could not confirm whether any are configured; the Google container needs to be reviewed. The providers, cookies and retention periods depend on the tags found there.

External services

Third-party content uses separate permissions so the site does not connect to each provider until you allow the relevant service. Allowing an integration lets the provider receive at least your IP address and technical request data. The provider may use its own cookies or similar technologies under its settings and policies.

Instagram / Meta

When you allow Instagram, the site requests post embed data from Meta and loads instagram.com/embed.js. The integration displays posts and is not automatically classified as advertising. Meta may apply its own technologies and purposes. Without permission, the site shows direct links and does not request the embedded content. See the Instagram privacy policy.

Facebook / Meta

Embedded Facebook videos load only after you accept the Facebook / Meta category. When they load, your browser requests the player from Facebook and shares technical data, including your IP address. Your choice is stored in local storage as part of your optional preferences. See the Meta privacy policy.

YouTube

Allowing YouTube creates a player using the domain youtube-nocookie.com. Enhanced Privacy Mode does not mean that no data is sent to or processed by Google. Without permission, the player is not created. See the Google privacy policy.

Google Maps

The interactive map remains blocked until you allow Google Maps. The code does not load the iframe or request its URL before that choice. Google receives the request when the map loads. See the Google privacy policy.

Interactive map (OpenStreetMap)

The facilities map remains blocked until you allow this category. Before you do, the site does not load Leaflet from the unpkg CDN or request tiles from OpenStreetMap. Once enabled, those providers receive your IP address and technical request data. If you withdraw permission, the map is hidden and future tile requests stop; connections already made cannot be undone. See the OpenStreetMap privacy policy.

Requests needed to display information

When you open the page, your browser requests weather and marine data from Open-Meteo and water-quality data from the European Environment Agency (EEA). These requests are needed to show the forecasts and data on the site and transmit technical connection data such as your IP address. The site also loads water-quality data files hosted with the page. These are not analytics tags. See the Open-Meteo information y la EEA legal information.

External links open only when you select them. The code does not connect to Google Analytics independently of GTM.

Changing your choice

You can accept, reject or choose categories in the initial notice. To reopen the panel, use the in the footer. Your choice is kept in this browser for up to six months, until you withdraw it or clear the site data. If the settings change substantially, a new choice is requested through a new consent version. Withdrawal prevents future loads from this page, but cannot delete a provider’s own cookies or undo data already sent.

You can also clear site data in your browser’s privacy settings. The controls vary by browser and version; consult its official help for guidance on managing cookies and site storage.
