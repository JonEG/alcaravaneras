Areas

The interactive map is provided by OpenStreetMap. When enabled, your browser requests Leaflet files from a CDN and map tiles from OpenStreetMap; your IP address and technical data are shared. See the OpenStreetMap privacy policy.

The sandy beach, beach-volleyball and canoeing areas come from OpenStreetMap geometry. Swimming and diving are shown with approximate icons on the water only; OSM provides no zones or boundaries for them. The icons do not mark official safety areas. Always follow signs and lifeguard instructions.
