A beach in the city

Las Alcaravaneras is a beach in Las Palmas de Gran Canaria. This guide brings together available local weather and sea information, plus directions to the beach.

Forecasts on this website come from weather and marine models. They may differ from conditions at the beach and do not replace official alerts or safety instructions.
