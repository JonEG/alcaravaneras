Accessibility at Las Alcaravaneras

Step-free routes, beach access platforms and how to check assisted bathing before your visit.

Explore access and facilities ↓

Accessible urban beach

Access to the promenade and beach

Las Alcaravaneras has accessible routes from the promenade and priority areas for people with reduced mobility (PRM).

The beach is wide, and distances across the sand can be considerable. Use our interactive facilities map to find accessible routes, the assisted-bathing point, toilets and showers.

What to expect

Accessibility and support

Equipment and staff availability may vary with the season, sea conditions and ongoing works.

01

Accessible routes and walkways

The municipal project includes four grooved, non-slip walkways across the sand to make it easier to reach the water. In 2026, plans were also announced for a shaded rest area prioritised for people with reduced mobility at the north access, along with ramps and seating improvements.

02

Assisted bathing

Ciudad de Mar provides information about assisted bathing and lists these contact numbers: 928 222 379 y 928 473 501. Call ahead to confirm whether the service is operating at Las Alcaravaneras, its hours, how to request it and what equipment is available.

03

Toilets and showers

Official tourism information lists accessible toilets and a priority area for people with reduced mobility. Ciudad de Mar says the bathing facility offers a service for visitors with reduced mobility and publishes daily hours of 11:00–18:00; check that these are still current before you go. The City Council has also announced upgrades to showers and foot-wash stations, so check which facilities are open.

Before you go

Check service availability

Assisted bathing and lifeguard cover may be seasonal. Ciudad de Mar publishes lifeguard periods and contact numbers for assisted bathing, but no specific hours for this support. Call before visiting to confirm availability, meeting point, whether you need to book and the conditions that day.

Published phone numbers: 928 222 379 · 928 473 501.
