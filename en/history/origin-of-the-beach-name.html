Las Alcaravaneras: where does our beach get its name?

The beach's name holds a clue to the sandy expanses that once lay here and the bird that may have lived among them.

Today, we know Las Alcaravaneras as an urban beach surrounded by buildings, avenues and boats. It is a place to walk, swim, play sports or simply sit and look out to sea.

But many years ago, it all looked very different. Where we now see streets and buildings, there were wide stretches of sand and stony ground. One special name has survived from that landscape: Las Alcaravaneras.

Where does the name come from? To find a possible answer, we need to meet a small inhabitant of those old sandy expanses: the stone-curlew.

A beach that used to be much larger

More than a hundred years ago, the name Alcaravaneras referred to more than the beach we know today. It was used for a much larger area of Santa Catalina, with sandy and stony ground.

Those open areas reached as far as places now known as Ciudad Jardín and Mesa y López. It is hard to picture, isn't it? The city was much smaller then, and the landscape was made up of open ground, dunes and paths.

Today's beach was part of a wider coastal area known as the Caleta de Santa Catalina. Over time, the city grew, the port changed, and new avenues and buildings were built. Much of that landscape gradually disappeared.

What the old sandy expanses looked like

Old photographs let us travel back in time and imagine what this part of the city looked like before it filled with buildings and roads.

The photograph gives us an idea of just how much the landscape has changed.

A name linked to a bird

The stone-curlew is a bird that lives in open, dry and stony places. It has large eyes and long legs. It can be easy to miss, but it is a fascinating bird.

Según explica el investigador Humberto Pérez Hidalgo en su libro Origins and stories of places in Gran Canaria, the stone-curlew once lived in this area. Its presence could explain the name Alcaravaneras.

We do not have an old document that tells us exactly how the name began, so this is a likely explanation, not an absolute certainty. It fits the landscape that existed at the time.

A name already in use more than 180 years ago

How do we know that the name Alcaravaneras was used so long ago?

The Provincial Historical Archive of Las Palmas preserves a description of a municipal file from 1841–1842. It mentions land in Alcaravaneras, in Santa Catalina, as well as an irrigation channel: a small canal that carried water to fields.

This shows that the name was already in use more than 180 years ago. It was probably known even earlier!

A clue from the past

Did you know…?

In the past, the name Alcaravaneras was used for an area much larger than today's beach. The name has survived the city's growth and still reminds us what this place was like many years ago!

When we walk along the beach today, we may not think about the old sandy expanses or the birds that lived there. But its name preserves a small clue to a landscape that has almost disappeared.
